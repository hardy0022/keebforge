Keyboard Switch Soldering

Soldering switches into a keyboard PCB is precise work — a bad joint means a dead key later. KeebForge solders every pin with clean 60/40 joints, and offers full builds from ₹500–550 for 60–65% boards and ₹650–800 for TKLs.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What this service is

Switch soldering installs switches into a keyboard PCB with clean, consistent joints. Every pin gets proper heat and a solid 60/40 solder joint, so the keyboard works reliably for years. Soldering is also the core of complete keyboard builds — a 60–65% build is ₹500–550 and a TKL is ₹650–800.
