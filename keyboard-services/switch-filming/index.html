Mechanical Keyboard Switch Filming

Switch filming is a thin plastic film placed between the top and bottom housings of a switch. It tightens the fit, reduces stem wobble and gives a deeper, more solid sound. KeebForge fits Durock films at ₹7 per switch or TX films at ₹9 per switch.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What this service is

Switch filming places a thin film between the top and bottom housings of a switch. It takes out the slack between the two halves, which means less stem wobble, a firmer feel and a deeper, more solid sound on every keystroke.

Frequently asked questions

What does a switch film do?
A switch film sits between the top and bottom switch housings, removing play between them. That means less stem wobble and a more solid, consistent typing feel and sound.
Which film should I pick?
Durock films (₹7 per switch) are the standard choice. TX films (₹9 per switch) offer a slightly heavier, firmer fit.
Is filming done with lubing?
Yes — filming pairs well with lubing. The complete mod combo includes lube, film and spring swap for ₹20 per switch.
Do I need films?
If your keycaps wobble visibly or your switches feel loose, films are the fix. They're also a popular finishing touch on custom builds.
