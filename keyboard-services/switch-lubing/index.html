Mechanical Keyboard Switch Lubing

Switches feel scratchy because the stem and spring slide on raw plastic. Lubing applies a thin layer of Krytox 205g0 to the moving parts of every switch, so each keypress feels smooth, consistent and quiet. KeebForge lubes individual switches by hand, at ₹12 per switch.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What this service is

Switch lubing is the application of a thin, even layer of Krytox 205g0 to the moving parts of each switch — the stem legs, rails and springs. It removes the scratchy, gritty feel of dry plastic-on-plastic contact and makes keystrokes smooth, consistent and quieter. It's the single most popular switch service for custom keyboard builders in India.

Frequently asked questions

What is keyboard switch lubing?
Switch lubing is the application of a thin, even layer of lubricant to the moving parts of each switch — the stem legs, rails and springs — so keys feel smooth and consistent instead of scratchy.
What lube do you use?
Krytox 205g0, a premium dielectric grease widely used for linear and tactile switch springs and housings.
How much does switch lubing cost?
Krytox 205g0 lubing is ₹12 per switch. Spring swap & oil is ₹3 per switch and switch stem tuning is ₹5 per switch.
Should I lube my switches?
If your switches feel scratchy, gritty or inconsistent, lubing is the most direct fix. Linear switches benefit most; tactile switches are lubed more sparingly.
Can I send only my switches?
Yes — you can ship just the switches. The buyer covers shipping in both directions.
How long does lubing take?
Turnaround is generally 5–7 days depending on order size and the queue.
