Hot-Swap Socket Repair

Hot-swap sockets take abuse: a pulled switch can lift the socket right off the PCB, killing that key. KeebForge replaces damaged hot-swap sockets at ₹13 per socket — new installs or replacements — and can also convert soldered boards using Mill-Max sockets.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What this service is

Hot-swap socket repair fixes sockets that have been lifted off the PCB, broken, or worn from repeated switch changes. When a socket fails, the key becomes dead or intermittent. KeebForge removes the damaged socket and installs a replacement at ₹13 per socket.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know a socket is damaged?
A key that's dead, intermittent, or works only when the switch is held at an angle usually points to a lifted or broken socket.
Can a damaged socket be replaced?
Yes. The damaged socket is removed and a new one installed at ₹13 per socket.
Can you make a soldered board hot-swap?
Yes — Mill-Max socket installation converts a soldered board to hot-swap at ₹18 per socket.
Do I send the whole keyboard?
Yes, or just the PCB if you prefer. Shipping is covered by the buyer in both directions.
