Custom Mechanical Keyboard Builds

A custom keyboard is your choice of case, PCB, switches, stabilizers and keycaps, assembled properly. KeebForge builds 60–65% boards from ₹500–550 and TKLs from ₹650–800, and quotes split and fully bespoke layouts individually.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What this service is

A custom keyboard build assembles your chosen case, PCB, plate, switches, stabilizers and keycaps into a finished keyboard. KeebForge handles the soldering, tuning and testing so it arrives working. Compact 60–65% builds start at ₹500–550, TKL builds at ₹650–800, and split or fully bespoke layouts are quoted individually.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom keyboard build cost?
A 60–65% build is ₹500–550 and a TKL build is ₹650–800 for assembly and soldering. Fully bespoke builds (case, PCB, layout) are quoted individually.
What's included in a build?
Assembly and soldering of the keyboard with your parts — PCB, plate, switches, stabilizers and keycaps. Optional switch lubing, filming and stabilizer tuning can be added.
What do I need to provide?
The parts. KeebForge can guide part selection and sourcing, and firmware upload and testing can be included.
Can you build a split keyboard?
Yes — split keyboard builds are quoted after review of the layout and parts.
How long does a build take?
Turnaround is generally 5–7 days depending on complexity and the queue.
