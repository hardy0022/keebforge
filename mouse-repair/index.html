Gaming Mouse Repair & Services

Gaming mice wear out in predictable ways: switches start double-clicking, scroll wheels skip or die, and feet lose their glide. KeebForge replaces switches, encoders and skates, and repairs cable, sensor and connectivity faults — by mail, from anywhere in India.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What mouse problems we fix

🎯

Double-clicking switches

Worn main switches register double clicks. Replaced on the left and right buttons.

🎡

Skipping or dead scroll wheel

A failing encoder causes inconsistent, reversed or dead scrolling. Replaced to match your model.

🔘

Mushy or dead side / middle buttons

Forward, back, DPI and scroll-click switches wear out and get replaced.

🛼

Worn out mouse feet

Old PTFE skates drag and scratch. Replaced and leveled for smooth glide.

🩺

Cable, sensor or connectivity issues

General diagnostics for buttons, sensor, cable and connectivity problems — quoted after inspection.

🧵

Hollow or rattly shell

Tape mod reduces shell flex, rattle and hollow acoustics.

02

Mouse switch replacement

The most common mouse repair in India is a double-clicking switch. Over time the microswitch contacts in the left or right click button wear out and register two clicks for one press. The fix is a clean switch swap — the worn switch is desoldered and replaced.

03

Encoder replacement (scroll wheel)

If your scroll wheel skips steps, scrolls the wrong direction, or does nothing at all, the rotary encoder is usually the culprit. KeebForge replaces the encoder with one sourced to match your mouse model.

04

Mouse mods

Beyond repairs, KeebForge also does quality-of-life mods: skate / feet replacement with fresh PTFE, and the tape mod to reduce shell flex, rattle and hollow acoustics.

Frequently asked questions

Do you repair gaming mice?
Yes. KeebForge repairs gaming mice — worn or double-clicking switches, dead scroll wheels, bad encoders, tired skates, and cable/sensor/connectivity problems.
Can you fix a mouse with double-clicking switches?
Yes. Double-clicking is almost always a worn switch. We replace the left or right click switches, or the middle and side button switches.
Can you replace a mouse encoder?
Yes. If the scroll wheel skips, scrolls backwards, or feels dead, the encoder is replaced with one matched to your mouse model.
Do you replace mouse feet / skates?
Yes. Fresh PTFE skates are fitted and leveled.
Do you need to ship the mouse?
Yes. KeebForge is a mail-in service from Jammu & Kashmir and accepts mice from anywhere in India. There is no physical walk-in shop.
How much does mouse repair cost?
Switch replacements, encoder swaps and other mouse work are quoted after inspection. Payment is confirmed before work begins.
How do I send my mouse for repair?
Place an order on the website, confirm the details, and ship the mouse to Jammu & Kashmir. The buyer covers shipping in both directions.
