Gaming Mouse Switch Replacement

A double-clicking mouse makes you misclick in games and waste work in apps. The fix is a clean switch swap: the worn microswitch is desoldered and replaced on the left or right button, or on middle and side buttons. KeebForge quotes the work after inspection.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What this service is

Mouse switch replacement fixes double-clicking, non-registering, or mushy buttons. The worn microswitch is desoldered and a new one fitted. You can bring your own switches or ask KeebForge to source them.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my mouse double-click?
The microswitch inside the left or right button wears out over time and registers two clicks for one press. The fix is replacing the switch.
How much does mouse switch replacement cost?
Mouse switch work is quoted after inspection — the mouse is assessed and a price confirmed before work starts.
Can you replace side and middle buttons too?
Yes — forward, back, DPI and scroll-click switches can all be replaced.
Do I need to provide switches?
No — bring your own switches or ask KeebForge to source them.
