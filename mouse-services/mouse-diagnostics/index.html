Mouse Diagnostics & Repair

If your mouse won't connect, the sensor glitches, buttons stop working, or the cable is damaged, KeebForge will diagnose it. General mouse diagnostics and repair covers cable, sensor, button and connectivity problems — quoted after inspection.

Mail-in service across India Turnaround: 5–7 days Payment before work begins
01

What this service is

General mouse diagnostics covers problems that don't fit a single part: cables that break or drop connection, sensors that glitch or track badly, buttons that stop responding, and mice that won't connect. The mouse is assessed and a quote given before any work.

Frequently asked questions

What does mouse diagnostics cover?
Cable, sensor, button and connectivity issues — plus any fault you can describe. The mouse is assessed and a quote given before work.
My mouse doesn't connect — can you fix it?
Possibly. Connection faults can come from the cable, the USB connector or the PCB. Diagnostics will find it.
How much does diagnostics cost?

Do you fix wired and wireless mice?
Diagnostics covers cable, sensor and connectivity issues; send the mouse and describe the fault to check whether it can be repaired.
